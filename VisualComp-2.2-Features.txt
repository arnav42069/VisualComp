VisualComp 2.2 — Azazel Audio
Product Page Content Brief

TAGLINE
A compressor that sees your mix. Multiband dynamics, a visual parametric EQ, and
mastering-grade output shaping, built into one plugin with a live, interactive graph
instead of a wall of knobs.

OVERVIEW
VisualComp 2.2 is a VST3 and Standalone audio plugin from Azazel Audio that merges a
full parametric EQ with per-band multiband compression and a broadband master
compressor, all controlled through one interactive frequency graph. Instead of
switching between separate EQ and compressor screens, engineers shape tone and
dynamics on the same curve: draw a node, hear it compress. It runs on Windows and Mac
inside any VST3 host, or standalone for quick auditioning.

CORE COMPRESSOR ENGINE
At its heart, VisualComp runs a broadband compressor with four selectable circuit
character models: VCA, FET, Opt(ical), and Tube. Each mode has its own attack/release
feel and harmonic behavior, from clean and fast (VCA) to warm and glued (Tube).
Standard controls: Threshold, Knee, Ratio, Attack, Release, Range, Makeup Gain, and a
wet/dry Mix knob for parallel-style blending. A three-mode output clipper (Soft,
Brickwall, or Off) sits after the compressor for final peak control, sharing one fixed
lookahead delay line across all three modes — switching clip styles live never
introduces clicks or changes the plugin's reported latency.

VISUAL, MULTIBAND-AWARE EQ
VisualComp's EQ is a full 8-node parametric equalizer — Bell, Low Shelf, High Shelf,
High-Pass, Low-Pass, and Notch filters, all built on industry-standard RBJ biquad
coefficients — drawn as a live, draggable curve rather than a numeric list. Nodes are
created with a double-click directly on the graph, dragged to set frequency and gain,
and shaped with mouse-wheel or an on-canvas "Dynamic Island" popup for Q, filter type,
and more.

What sets it apart: any EQ node can be linked to the compressor. A linked node gets
its own bandpass-filtered detector and becomes a genuine, independent dynamics band —
not just a filter, but a target for compression that reacts only to the energy in
that frequency range. New nodes are linked to the compressor by default, so sculpting
a frequency automatically gives you a dynamics band to control it with.

PER-BAND DYNAMICS, FABFILTER PRO-MB STYLE
Every linked band carries its own Threshold, Knee, Ratio, Range, Attack, and Release —
editable live from the main editor's Dynamics pane whenever a band is selected, or
from the floating Dynamic Island docked to the node itself. The Threshold knob uses a
logarithmic taper from 0 dB down to -60 dB, centered so the knob's halfway point lands
on a musically useful -20 dB — giving fine control across the everyday -20..0 dB
working range while still reaching deep, aggressive settings without the knob feeling
cramped. A Range control (+/-30 dB) sets how far a band's dynamics can swing the gain,
and its sign chooses the direction: negative for classic downward compression,
positive to automatically flip the band into upward mode — boosting quiet material
below the threshold instead of squashing loud material above it, useful for pulling up
buried low-level detail (room tone, sustain tails, quiet vocal phrases) without
touching anything already loud. Each band's progress meters update live and in real
time as knobs move, whether edited from the main panel or the node's own popup.

Frequency range markers (the min/max edges of each node's active band) snap together
automatically when dragged within 100 Hz of a neighboring band's edge, so adjacent
bands can be lined up edge-to-edge with sample-accurate precision instead of guessing
by eye.

METERING
VisualComp includes peak and RMS meters with musical ballistics, plus an approximate
LUFS loudness reading (a continuous, EMA-smoothed K-weighting approximation) to give a
fast, glanceable loudness read while mixing — clearly labeled as approximate rather
than a certified BS.1770 delivery measurement, so it's a mixing aid, not a delivery
spec tool. A dedicated Curve/GR panel shows the live transfer curve and gain-reduction
activity, collapsible to keep the interface compact when it's not needed.

INTERFACE & WORKFLOW
The interface is dark-themed (near-black background, warm orange accent) with a clean,
modern look built around waveform displays for input and output signal, a large
central EQ/dynamics graph, and docked side panels that expand only when needed — the
EQ panel and the Curve/GR panel each slide out from their own edge without disturbing
the rest of the layout. Every EQ node's full state (frequency, gain, Q, filter type,
linked/unlinked, and its own dynamics settings) is saved with your session or exported
as a standalone preset, independent of the host's automation lanes — so preset recall
and DAW automation never conflict with each other.

PRESETS
VisualComp ships with a curated factory preset library organized by use case —
Mastering (glue, warmth, mix-bus punch and smoothing), Drums (kick punch, snare crack,
room crush, overhead taming, percussion control), Vocals (smooth leads, upfront leads,
thickening, backing-vocal tightening, whisper/breath boosting), Bass, and more —
covering both transparent, corrective use and deliberately colored, characterful
settings. Presets are a starting point tuned for real mixing scenarios, not just
factory-default knob positions. Users can also save and recall their own custom
presets from a dedicated user preset folder.

WHO IT'S FOR
Mixing and mastering engineers who want to shape tone and control dynamics in the same
gesture — sculpting a frequency and immediately deciding how it should behave
dynamically, without tabbing between an EQ plugin and a compressor plugin and
losing the mental model of the mix in between. Its visual, node-based workflow suits
producers who think in curves and shapes as much as in numbers.

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS (for headline/hero copy)
- One graph, two jobs: draw the EQ curve and get real per-band compression for free.
- Four compressor characters (VCA/FET/Opt/Tube) in one broadband stage.
- Musically-tapered Threshold knob — precision where you actually work, -20..0 dB.
- Upward or downward per band, chosen by a single Range knob's sign.
- Zero-latency-change output clipping — switch Soft/Brickwall/Off live, mid-mix.
- Built-in approximate LUFS reading alongside peak/RMS.
- Genre-organized factory preset library for Mastering, Drums, Vocals, Bass, and more.
- VST3 + Standalone, Windows and Mac.
